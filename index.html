Your four-week workout & dance programme

A beginner-friendly plan built around your Tuesday and Saturday swimming lessons. Keep the pace comfortable; you can shorten, swap or skip a session. Tick each session when you complete it.

Start each active session with 3–5 minutes of easy movement and finish with 3–5 minutes of gentle cool-down. During swimming lessons, follow your instructor and take breaks whenever needed. Stop if you feel unwell, dizzy or have pain.

Dance styles to try

Low-impact Afrobeats

Step-touch side to side, gentle shoulder rolls, alternating toe taps and easy arm waves. Keep both feet close to the floor.

Gospel praise dance

March gently in place, step forward and back, sway side to side and add relaxed claps or arm raises.

Gentle freestyle

Choose a favourite song. Repeat comfortable side steps, heel digs and slow turns. Leave out jumps and fast pivots.

Portion guide

• Protein: a palm-sized serving of fish or chicken, two eggs, or a cupped handful of beans.

• Rice, yam, plantain or swallow: start with a fist-sized portion and adjust to your appetite.

• Vegetables: aim to make them a generous part of your plate.

• Add satisfying fats and eat slowly. You do not need to count every bite.

These are flexible, general meal-planning ideas—not medical nutrition advice. Adjust portions to your needs and any advice from your healthcare professional.
